## Don't pay mobile rates for a datacenter job

The fastest way to overpay isn't a missing coupon — it's buying the wrong lane. The price gap between proxy types reflects how hard the IP is to source, not how good it is.

**Residential at $1/GB** routes through real home connections. Use it for targets that actually inspect IP reputation: e-commerce sites, SERPs, social platforms, price monitoring. Country targeting is included.

**Datacenter at $0.50/GB** is the cheapest lane and the right one for high-volume work on sites that don't scrutinize server IPs — public databases, news sites, broad crawling. Half the residential rate for the same volume is real money.

**Mobile at $2/GB** is for jobs where cellular IPs change the outcome: app testing, mobile-first platforms, and anti-bot systems that specifically flag non-mobile traffic patterns.

**Premium residential at $5/GB** sits on a higher-quality sub-pool with its own account manager and all targeting options included. It's for targets where the standard residential lane isn't clearing.

Most first purchases should be residential or datacenter. Paying $2/GB for work that would have been fine on a $0.50 lane is the single most expensive mistake in proxy budgeting.

## What quietly adds to the bill

Three things to know before checkout, because none of them appear in the headline rate.

**Advanced targeting costs extra on the standard residential lane.** Country selection is included in the base rate. State, city, ZIP and ASN filters are billed on top — independent breakdowns of the pricing page put them at roughly double the standard per-GB rate on residential. Datacenter plans list finer targeting as included, so the treatment isn't identical across lanes. If your project depends on city or ZIP precision, budget for it and confirm the current billing with support rather than assuming.

**Sticky sessions default to 30 minutes.** Rotating connections run on port 823 for HTTP/HTTPS and 824 for SOCKS5, with a new IP per request. Sticky sessions bind an IP to a port in the 10000–20000 range, and if you don't specify an interval, the default is 30 minutes. Long-lived workflows — account logins, carts, session-dependent scraping — need to expect that rotation rather than assume persistence.

**There's no free tier.** No free trial, no free monthly allowance, no 1,000 requests on the house. The $5 pack is the entry point, and for most people that's a better deal than a trial anyway, since the traffic doesn't expire if you don't finish it.
